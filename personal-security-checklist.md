# Ways to improve my online privacy
1. Use strong, unique passwords for every account.
2. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible.
3. Use a password manager to securely store passwords.
4. Keep my phone, computer, apps, and browsers updated to fix security vulnerabilities.
5. Limit the personal information I share online, such as my address, phone number, and location.
6. Review and restrict app permissions, especially access to my location, camera, microphone, and contacts.
7. Use a privacy-focused browser and block unnecessary trackers and cookies.
8. Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive activities or use a trusted VPN when necessary.
9. Review privacy settings on social media and make personal posts visible only to people I trust.
10. Be cautious of phishing emails, messages, and suspicious links before clicking or providing information.
